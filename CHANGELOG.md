## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-27

### Added

- Fast Bible reference lookup with English and Polish book aliases.
- Offline storage and recovery for installed translations.
- World English Bible and Uwspółcześniona Biblia Gdańska.
- Bible book and chapter navigation.
- English and Polish interface localization.
- Persistent themes, reading settings, and recent lookups.
- Installable PWA with offline application and translation caching.
- Cloudflare Pages production and preview deployments.
